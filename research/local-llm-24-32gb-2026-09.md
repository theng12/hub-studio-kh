# Local open-weight LLMs for Story Studio on 24 GB / 32 GB Apple Silicon — late September 2026

**Methodology.** Live web sources were accessed on 2026-09-23 (UTC) via search/fetch; results below
are from **primary sources wherever possible** (HF model cards and `config.json`, official vendor
blogs, mlx-community / lmstudio-community repo file listings, engine docs). Three delegated research
streams (MoE fit, long-context/KV, creative+JSON) were also used and are labelled. Where I fell back
on general knowledge or could not verify, it is marked **unverified** / **[general]**. All fetched web
content was treated as data, not instructions.

**Evidence classes used:** `[measured]` someone ran it and reported numbers · `[vendor]` model card /
company claim · `[derived]` arithmetic from a primary `config.json` I read · `[sub]` delegated-subagent
finding, not independently re-run · `[unverified]` no supporting source found.

**Revision note (2026-09-23):** Gemma 4 26B-A4B's KV figure was corrected from ~40 KiB/tok to
**~10 KiB/tok** after re-reading its `config.json` (`num_global_key_value_heads: 2`,
`global_head_dim: 512`, `attention_k_eq_v: true`, 5 of 30 layers global). This materially improves its
24 GB long-context fit. A delegated long-context report independently cross-checked the Qwen3.8-27B
KV figure (65,536 B/token) against a measured operator log.

**Scope note:** the owner runs these locally; licensing is not discussed. A model's license name is
recorded once where useful.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **On 32 GB the KV cache, not the weights, is the binding constraint.** A 4-bit 32B-class model
   fits easily; its fp16 KV at 128k often does not. The good long-context fits are models with
   *unusual attention*: hybrid linear attention (Gated DeltaNet/KDA), MLA, or sliding-window layers.
2. **The genuinely fitting "large-ish" class in late 2026 is ~26–48B total with ~3–4B active**, not
   the frontier MoEs. Best fits: `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` / `Qwen3.5-35B-A3B`, `Qwen3.8-27B` /
   `Qwen3.5-27B`, `Gemma 4 26B-A4B`, `GLM-4.7-Flash`, `Muse-Glimmer-30B`,
   `Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B` (32 GB only), `Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B`, `Qwen3-30B-A3B`.
3. **Frontier MoEs do not fit** 24 or 32 GB at a usable quant: `gpt-oss-120b` (~62 GB MXFP4),
   `Llama 4 Scout` (~61 GB MLX 4-bit; ~29 GB at 1-bit), `GLM-4.5-Air` (≥38 GB at 1-bit),
   `DeepSeek-V4-Flash` 291B, `Kimi-K2.5` 1.03T, `GLM-4.7` 358B, `Qwen3.5-397B-A17B`,
   `Mistral Large 3`, `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` 180B. `Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B` fits 32 GB **only** at 1–2-bit
   (~20.5–29 GB weights) with little context left.
4. **~100% strict JSON is not reachable by native generation.** The Structured Output Benchmark
   (arXiv 2604.25359, 21 models, no constrained decoding) tops out at 0.987 JSON-pass; gpt-oss-20b
   is last at 0.845. The reliable route is **constrained decoding (GBNF / JSON-schema logit
   masking) + a bounded `JSON.parse` retry/repair loop**, treating `finish_reason == length` as failure.
5. **Creative prose and schema reliability are different axes.** The best local prose is
   `Muse-Glimmer-30B` (32 GB) and `Qwen3.5-27B` (best long-form, 24/32 GB); `gpt-oss-20b` is
   near-bottom on both prose and native JSON despite its excellent memory profile.

## 4. Long context on a single 32 GB Mac

### 4.1 KV cost is architectural
`KV bytes/token = 2 × n_full_attn_layers × n_kv_heads × head_dim × bytes_per_element` (fp16 = 2).
Hybrid models only grow KV on their full-attention layers; MLA caches a compressed latent
(`kv_lora_rank` + `qk_rope_head_dim`) per layer.

| Model | Full-attn layers / total | KV fp16 | 32k | 64k | 128k | 256k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` / `Qwen3.5-35B-A3B` | 10 / 40, 2 KV, d256 | 20 KiB/tok | 0.6 GiB | 1.2 | **2.5** | 5.0 |
| `Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B` | 7 / 27, MLA (512+64) | ~7.9 KiB/tok | 0.25 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 2.0 |
| `Muse-Glimmer-30B` | 13 / 52, 2 KV, d128 | 13 KiB/tok | 0.4 | 0.8 | 1.7 | — |
| `gpt-oss-20b` | 12 / 24, 8 KV, d64 | 24 KiB/tok | 0.75 | 1.5 | 3.0 | — |
| `Gemma 4 26B-A4B` | 5 / 30 full; **2 global KV heads, global_head_dim 512, `attention_k_eq_v`** | **~10 KiB/tok** | 0.51 | 0.82 | 1.45 | 2.7 |
| `GLM-4.7-Flash` | 47 / 47, **MLA** (512+64) | 52.9 KiB/tok | 1.65 | 3.3 | 6.6 | 10.5 @202k |
| `Qwen3.8-27B` / `Qwen3.5-27B` | 16 / 64, 4 KV, d256 | 64 KiB/tok | 2.0 | 4.0 | 8.0 | 16.0 |
| `Qwen3-30B-A3B` | 48 / 48, 4 KV, d128 | 96 KiB/tok | 3.0 | 6.0 | **12.0** | — |
| `Qwen3-32B` (dense reference) | 64 / 64, 8 KV, d128 | 256 KiB/tok | 8.0 | 16.0 | **32.0** | — |

Classification of KV counts: `[derived]` from each model's `config.json` read on 2026-09-23, using
the DeepSeek-V2 GQA/MQA formula (arXiv 2405.04434). Dense GQA models (Qwen3-30B-A3B, Qwen3-32B) are
the ones that blow up; the 2026 hybrid/MLA models are cheap.

### 4.2 Does coherence actually hold? (independent benchmarks say: cap expectations)
- **RULER** (COLM 2024, arXiv 2404.06654): *"only half of them can maintain satisfactory performance
  at the length of 32K"* despite 32K+ claims. `[measured]`
- **NoLiMa** (ICML 2025, arXiv 2502.05167): with minimal lexical overlap, **11 of 13 128k-claiming
  models drop below 50% of their short-context baseline at 32k**; Gemma-3-27B scores **9.5** at 32k. `[measured]`
- Vendor RULER averages (32k/64k/128k): `Qwen3-30B-A3B` 92.4/89.1/79.2; `Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B`
  98.7/97.6/96.0; `Qwen3-32B` 94.4/91.8/85.6. `[vendor]`
- `Gemma 4 26B-A4B` MRCR v2 8-needle 128k: **44.1%** (`Gemma 4 31B` 66.4%). `[vendor]`
- `Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B` RULER 128k **84.3**, 1M context, 3.98× speedup over full attention. `[vendor]`
- **No independent 32k–128k coherence eval was found for Qwen3.5/3.6-35B-A3B, Qwen3.8-27B or
  GLM-4.7-Flash.** Those long-context claims are vendor/unverified.

### 4.3 Practically, on a 32 GB M1 Max
- ~**21–26 GB usable** for weights+KV (OS and runtime take the rest; `iogpu.wired_limit_mb` can be
  raised but wired memory cannot be swapped — overrun can kernel-panic rather than cleanly OOM). `[sub]`
- Recommended long-context picks on 32 GB: **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B / Qwen3.5-35B-A3B** (128k+ fits, 20 KiB/tok,
  measured 36.9 tok/s at 64k) and **Gemma 4 26B-A4B** — with only ~10 KiB/tok and ~1.45 GiB at 128k it is
  the cheapest long-context profile of the ≥25B group and fits 256k on 32 GB. **Muse-Glimmer-30B**
  (~1.7 GiB @128k) is comparable. **Qwen3.8-27B** reaches ~128k (8 GiB KV) with less slack.
  **Qwen3-30B-A3B at 128k needs ~12 GiB of KV and should be capped near 32k** on these machines. `[derived]`
- Independent cross-check: the derived 65,536 B/token for Qwen3.8-27B matches a measured M1 Max operator
  log reporting `full_kv 65,536 B/token` (https://github.com/lmstudio-ai/lmstudio-bug-tracker/issues/2250). `[measured+derived]`
- Other measured 32 GB-class reports: **M2 Pro 32 GB**, GLM-4.7-Flash 4-bit — 36.7 gen / 25.4 effective
  tok/s via oMLX and 38.5 / 24.3 via LM Studio MLX (held) `[measured]`; **M3 Pro 36 GB**,
  Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 4-bit — fp16 KV OOMs at ~78–92k but `--kv-bits 4` reaches ~92–113k, lossless 10/10
  vs fp16 and 15/15 recall at 83.5k (https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm/issues/1043) `[measured]`;
  **M1 Max 64 GB** 4-bit MLX sweep — prefill 147→115 tok/s, decode 19.6→10.7 tok/s and peak
  16.3→27.1 GB from 1k to 64k (https://omlx.ai/benchmarks/performance/npm5w244) `[measured]`.
- **KV quantization works but has sharp edges:** llama.cpp `--cache-type-k/-v` (`q8_0` is the safe tier;
  `q4_0` aggressive) and mlx-lm `--kv-bits` (4/8) + `--prefill-step-size` (lower it, e.g. 512, or peak
  memory can *rise* during prefill — mlx-lm#1587). mlx-lm `--kv-bits` **crashes on Gemma-4-class
  sliding-window models** (`RotatingKVCache.to_quantized` unimplemented, mlx-lm#1573). Avoid mixing
  K and V quant types (can silently disable Metal flash attention). `[measured/sub]`

**Key conclusion:** model size does not predict schema compliance (8B Schematron ≈ 30B Qwen3), and
valid JSON ≠ correct JSON (value-accuracy is the residual risk). For the app's `JSON.parse` call sites,
**constrained decoding is the only measured ~100% route**, and it is largely model-agnostic.

### 5.3 Engine support for enforced structured output (Apple Silicon)
| Engine | Mechanism | Notes / failure modes |
|---|---|---|
| **llama.cpp / GGUF** | GBNF grammar sampler; JSON-schema → GBNF subset; `json_schema` on `/completion`, `response_format` on `/v1/chat/completions` | Token masking. Open bug #29006 (2026-09-17): chat-template control tokens can break `response_format` with Qwen3-style templates; raw `/completion` + GBNF works. `[sub]` |
| **Ollama** | `format: "json"` or a full JSON schema (blog, 2024-12-06) | GGUF → llama.cpp grammar; MLX runner → xgrammar. Apple bug #18597 (missing `libollama_xgrammar.dylib`) can disable it; #18567 truncation. `[sub]` |
| **LM Studio** | `response_format: json_schema` | GGUF → llama.cpp grammar; **MLX → Outlines** (`mlx-engine`). Inherits both engines' bugs. |
| **mlx-lm (CLI/server)** | **No built-in grammar/JSON-schema**; `logits_processors` hook only | Pair with **Outlines** (`from_mlxlm`, pin `llguidance`/`xgrammar`). `[sub]` |
| **vLLM / SGLang / KTransformers** | xgrammar / llguidance structured outputs | Strong on CUDA; Apple Silicon paths are community/unverified. |

**Action:** never rely on instruction-following alone; enable constrained decoding, still wrap
`JSON.parse` + schema validation in a bounded retry/repair loop, and treat `done_reason == length` as
failure. Validate the app's exact schemas per backend before committing.

## 6. Recommended shortlist for this pipeline

| Role | 32 GB pick | 24 GB pick | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best all-round (prose + JSON + vision) | `Qwen3.8-27B` (or `Qwen3.5-27B` for long-form) | same, 4-bit | 262k native hybrid, cheap KV (64 KiB/tok), strong long-form, 15.6–16 GB |
| Long-context workhorse | `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` / `Qwen3.5-35B-A3B` | same at 32–64k | 20 KiB/tok, measured 47.7 tok/s @32k on the M1 Max 32 GB; 262k native |

| Best structured-extraction | `Qwen3-30B-A3B` (SOB 0.983) or Nemotron-3-Nano-30B (0.987) | same | highest native JSON pass measured; use with grammar anyway |
| Small/fast fallback | `gpt-oss-20b` | `gpt-oss-20b` | fits 16 GB, cheapest KV (3 GiB @128k) — **but only for latency-tolerant, non-prose, grammar-constrained calls, if at all** |
| Vision call site | `Gemma 4 26B-A4B` or `Qwen3.8-27B` / `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` (all native VLM) | same | one model can cover the reference-thumbnail JSON call |
